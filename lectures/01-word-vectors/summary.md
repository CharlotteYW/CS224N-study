# Summary: Word vectors

Status: not started

## Why this unit exists

The course starts by replacing a word-as-symbol with a word-as-vector. Everything later (contextual states, attention, transformers) is a more careful version of this move.

## Concepts you must be able to explain

- The distributional hypothesis: a word's meaning shows up in the words around it
- One-hot vectors versus a dense distributed representation
- Skip-gram: a center word predicts its context words
- Two vectors per word type: a center vector and an outside vector
- The softmax over the whole vocabulary, and why that sum is expensive
- Gradient descent as the way the vectors move
