Week 14: study attention and Transformer blocks, with pauses for shapes and masks. The verified recording is Winter 2023.

## Why this is in the course

The transformer removes recurrence and keeps attention, applied to every position. This is the model the rest of the course, and Assignment 4, are about.

## Done when

You can write scaled dot-product attention from memory, including the softmax over keys.
