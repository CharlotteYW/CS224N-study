# Summary: Responsible and human-centered NLP

## Why this unit exists

A model that scores well can still be the wrong system to deploy. This guest lecture is the course's pass over that gap. It will not teach you a new architecture.

## Concepts you must be able to explain

- A measured score is not a complete description of harm
- Who is in the evaluation, and who is left out
- What you would want to know before calling your project finished
