## Why this is in the course

Before transformers, two other inductive biases were serious contenders for sentences: convolution over nearby words, and a recursive net that follows a parse tree. Seeing them makes the transformer's choice legible.

## Done when

Optional. If you watch it, summary.md says what convolution and tree recursion each assume.
