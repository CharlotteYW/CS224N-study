# Summary: ConvNets, tree recursive networks, and constituency parsing

Status: Not Started

## Why this unit exists

Before transformers, two other inductive biases were serious contenders for sentences: convolution over nearby words, and a recursive net that follows a parse tree. Seeing them makes the transformer's choice legible.

## Concepts you must be able to explain

- A convolutional filter as a local n-gram detector
- A tree RNN as composition along a constituency tree
- What each bias assumes about language, and what self-attention refuses to assume
