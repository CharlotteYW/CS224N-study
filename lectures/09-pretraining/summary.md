# Summary: Pretraining

Status: not started

## Why this unit exists

Word2vec trained a vector and stopped. Pretraining trains a whole network on a huge amount of text, then that network is reused. The objective you choose (masked versus next-word) decides what the network is good at.

## Concepts you must be able to explain

- Contextual representations: the vector for a word now depends on the sentence
- Masked language modeling (BERT) versus causal language modeling (GPT)
- What a pretext task is, and what fine-tuning changes afterward
- Why the static vectors from week 1 cannot do what a pretrained transformer does
