## Why this is in the course

A guest lecture on decoding attempted speech from neural recordings. It is here because the 2024 course included it, and because it shows sequence modeling outside of text on the web.

## Done when

A short summary exists if you watched it. Skipping it does not leave a hole in Assignments 1–4.
